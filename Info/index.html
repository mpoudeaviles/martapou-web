Hi! Nice to meet you


Let me introduce myself:

During the last few years at university I have created projects that have helped me develop a critical eye, attention to the user and aesthetic sensitivity. On my own, I have been participating whenever I can in group projects, from things related to music and its visual communication to event organization.

I consider that my strengths when approaching a project are transversality, organizational capacity and ease and curiosity in learning. I enjoy working in a team and feel especially comfortable in organizational or conceptualization roles. I am looking to take part in projects where I can apply my skills and evolve them, with a great interest in those related to interactive installations or creative direction.

Extra facts:

Beyond design, I love…
·Music, listening and making it.
·Taking pictures, especially of friends and events.
·Makeup and fashion, particularly into DIY culture.

And what about you?

I am always looking to participate in new projects! If you like what you see, you can contact me through instagram or mail.
