Interaction designer working with light, electronics and space.

I’m Marta, based in Barcelona. I design and build interactive installations where light, sound and people respond to each other, from the first idea and prototypes to the electronics, the programming and the setup on site.

I work as a designer at Estudi Antoni Arola, and I also develop my own projects and technical commissions.
