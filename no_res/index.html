2. no_res: the reverberation of the fyp

Reactive installation fueled by tiktok’s fyp


Images have escaped the screen. They encapsulate our attention, already monetized, through an algorithm designed to hold us captive. But could we inhabit them differently?

“No_res” is a reactive installation fueled by TikTok’s fyp. Using a rotating cloud of mirrors, generative audio, and real-time projection, we create an immersive landscape that only becomes corrupted by the presence of phones. Images are distorted, transformed into a liminal space where the digital no longer demands but floats.



Final master degree project
BAU, master in Audiovisual Innovation and interactive Installations, 2025
Work by Alex Sánchez, Ana Soriano and Marta Pou de Avilés.
